We identify international collaborators, contact them and arrange the first meetings. The work runs from understanding the group to sitting both sides down.

What we do

We help groups, departments and institutions find partners that complement their science: for joint research or for a specific call.

How

  1. Profile. An initial meeting and a definition of the collaborator you need.
  2. Mapping. A ranked list of groups or organisations, with a profile and contacts.
  3. Contact. Introduction, follow-up and first meetings. We join the first meeting.

An assignment usually takes about twelve weeks, with an update every two weeks. You receive the list, a short process summary and the meetings that could be arranged.

Who it is for

Research groups, departments and project offices. Also European consultancies that need partners outside their usual network.

Who we are

A team used to setting up scientific collaborations. Part of the team is based in Spain; we work with institutions in several regions.

Track record

  • Taken part in and led more than 30 competitive projects of different scales.
  • Attracted direct and indirect funding in demanding calls.
  • Developed tools, methods and resources used by other teams and institutions.
  • Ongoing collaboration with companies through transfer contracts and consulting.
  • Supervised several doctoral theses, including industry-linked projects.
  • Scientific output of more than 200 publications, plus patents and licences.
  • Public outreach, including a podcast with more than 330 episodes.
