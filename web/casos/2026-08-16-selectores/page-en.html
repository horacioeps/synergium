What we offer

Synergium helps researchers, groups, institutions and companies find reliable partners, structure international collaborations and build solid consortia for competitive projects.

Our work includes:

  • Identifying academic and industrial collaborators that fit each project.
  • Direct introductions and first meetings.
  • Support in finding and choosing national and international calls.
  • Early-stage proposal support: structure, partner alignment and strategy.
  • Additional services such as project podcasts, scientific communication and tailored micro-consortia.

The aim is to make partner search simpler, reduce uncertainty and raise the odds that each initiative succeeds.

Who we are

Synergium is a team with international experience in research and collaboration. Part of the core team is based in Spain, and we work regularly with institutions and professionals in several regions.

We are a multidisciplinary team used to connecting people from different fields, making collaboration workable and supporting complex projects. We do not present ourselves as individuals, but as a cohesive team with strategic view, practical experience and an established international network.

Track record

The Synergium team has:

  • Taken part in and led more than 30 competitive projects of different scales.
  • Attracted direct and indirect funding in demanding calls.
  • Developed tools, methods and resources used by other teams and institutions.
  • Ongoing collaboration with companies through transfer contracts and consulting.
  • Supervised several doctoral theses, including industry-linked projects.
  • Scientific output of more than 200 publications, plus patents and licences.
  • Public outreach, including a podcast with more than 330 episodes.

These results show we can connect, structure and strengthen scientific and technological collaborations with real impact.
